CORE TEAM RULEBOOK & CONSTITUTION

Motto

Build. Contribute. Collaborate.

1. VISION

FOSS Club IIIT Kalyani is a student-led community dedicated to promoting the use, development, contribution, and evangelism of Free and Open Source Software.

The club aims to create an environment where students can learn by building, contribute to real-world open-source projects, collaborate across technical domains, and create initiatives that benefit the wider community.

The club believes that:

Technology is best learned by building, strengthened by collaboration, and sustained through contribution.

2. OBJECTIVES

FOSS Club IIIT Kalyani shall work towards:

  • Promoting awareness and adoption of FOSS.
  • Encouraging students to contribute to existing open-source projects.
  • Building and maintaining open-source projects.
  • Conducting workshops, hackathons, technical sessions, and contribution drives.
  • Creating opportunities for peer-to-peer learning.
  • Encouraging collaboration between different technical domains.
  • Connecting students with the wider open-source ecosystem.
  • Developing a sustainable open-source culture at IIIT Kalyani.
  • Providing students with opportunities to take ownership of meaningful technical and community initiatives.

3. CORE VALUES

3.1 Open Knowledge

Knowledge should be shared whenever reasonably possible.

3.2 Contribution Over Titles

A Core Team position represents responsibility and ownership, not merely a designation.

3.3 Build

Members should be encouraged to turn ideas into working projects.

3.4 Collaborate

Teams should work together rather than operating as isolated departments.

3.5 Learn & Teach

Members are encouraged to learn from others and share their own knowledge.

3.6 Respect

Every member should be treated with professionalism and respect regardless of technical ability, year, role, or experience.

3.7 Open-Source Ethics

The club shall respect software licenses, attribution, intellectual property, privacy, and responsible security practices.

6. CLUB LEAD — AYUSH LAHIRI

5.1 Role

The Lead is responsible for the overall direction, coordination, continuity, and development of FOSS Club IIIT Kalyani.

Responsibilities

The Lead shall:

  • Define the overall vision and long-term direction of the club.
  • Coordinate all Core Team departments.
  • Ensure that activities remain aligned with the club's FOSS mission.
  • Approve major club initiatives.
  • Coordinate with institute authorities when necessary.
  • Represent FOSS Club IIIT Kalyani in official matters.
  • Oversee major events and initiatives.
  • Resolve major inter-team conflicts.
  • Ensure accountability across the Core Team.
  • Facilitate leadership transitions.
  • Maintain continuity between successive Core Teams.
  • Identify new opportunities for projects, collaborations, and community growth.

Authority

The Lead may:

  • Assign and reassign responsibilities.
  • Create temporary project teams.
  • Appoint project coordinators.
  • Call Core Team meetings.
  • Approve major initiatives.
  • Reorganize responsibilities when necessary.
  • Take temporary decisions during urgent situations.

Whenever practical, major decisions should be discussed with the relevant Core Team members before implementation.

15. CROSS-TEAM COLLABORATION

The departments are not independent organizations.

Major projects may involve multiple teams.

For example:

AI/ML + App/Web + DevOps + UI/UX

may collaborate on an AI-powered application.

Similarly:

Cybersecurity + Linux/DevOps + App/Web

may collaborate on a secure software or infrastructure project.

The Lead may create temporary Project Squads containing members from multiple departments.

Each major Project Squad should have:

  • Objective.
  • Project Lead.
  • Contributors.
  • Timeline.
  • Repository/documentation.
  • Completion criteria.

16. PROJECT DEVELOPMENT RULES

Projects developed under FOSS Club IIIT Kalyani should follow good open-source practices.

Version Control

Projects should use Git or an appropriate version-control system.

Documentation

Significant projects should include:

  • Project description.
  • Setup instructions.
  • Usage instructions.
  • Contributors.
  • License.
  • Known limitations where appropriate.

Licensing

Projects intended for open-source release should use an appropriate open-source license.

Dependencies and external projects must be used according to their licenses.

Attribution

External libraries, datasets, projects, and contributors must be credited appropriately.

17. CONTRIBUTION CULTURE

The club encourages two forms of contribution:

Internal Contribution

Working on projects created by FOSS Club IIIT Kalyani.

External Contribution

Contributing to established open-source projects.

Members should be encouraged to follow:

Discover → Understand → Discuss → Implement → Review → Contribute

The club values meaningful contributions over artificial activity.

  • Complete assigned follow-ups.

20. DECISION-MAKING

The club follows:

Discuss → Evaluate → Decide → Execute → Review

Technical Decisions

Technical decisions should primarily be handled by the relevant Technical Lead and project contributors.

Cross-Team Decisions

Relevant Leads should collaborate.

Club-Wide Decisions

Major club-wide decisions should involve the Lead and relevant Core Team members.

Final Responsibility

The Lead has final responsibility for resolving unresolved disputes and decisions affecting the overall direction of FOSS Club IIIT Kalyani.

21. CORE TEAM ACCOUNTABILITY

Every Core Team member is expected to:

  • Participate actively.
  • Complete assigned responsibilities.
  • Communicate delays early.
  • Attend important meetings.
  • Support other teams.
  • Maintain professional conduct.
  • Mentor newer members.
  • Take ownership of assigned work.
  • Contribute beyond their title when necessary.

A Core Team position is a responsibility, not merely a resume designation.

22. CODE OF CONDUCT

Members must:

  • Treat others respectfully.
  • Avoid harassment and discrimination.
  • Avoid bullying and intimidation.
  • Respect personal boundaries.
  • Give constructive criticism.
  • Credit others for their work.
  • Avoid plagiarism.
  • Respect intellectual property.
  • Maintain professionalism during events and collaborations.

Technical disagreement is acceptable.

Personal attacks are not.

23. ETHICAL & LEGAL RESPONSIBILITY

All club activities must comply with applicable laws, institute regulations, and platform policies.

Members must:

  • Obtain authorization for security testing.
  • Handle personal data responsibly.
  • Protect credentials.
  • Respect intellectual property.
  • Avoid malicious software distribution.
  • Avoid abuse of external services.

Technical capability does not automatically justify using a technique.

24. ACCESS & CREDENTIAL SECURITY

Members with access to club infrastructure or accounts are responsible for protecting that access.

Members must not:

  • Share passwords unnecessarily.
  • Commit secrets to public repositories.
  • Access another member's account without authorization.
  • Transfer administrative access without approval.
  • Store sensitive credentials publicly.

When someone leaves a privileged role, their access should be reviewed and transferred appropriately.

25. CORE TEAM PERFORMANCE

Performance should not be measured solely through:

  • Number of commits.
  • Number of posts.
  • Number of events.
  • Number of hours spent.

The club should consider:

  • Quality.
  • Reliability.
  • Ownership.
  • Initiative.
  • Collaboration.
  • Mentorship.
  • Impact.
  • Completion of responsibilities.

Members facing genuine difficulties should be supported where possible.

26. CORE TEAM MEMBERSHIP

A Core Team member may be asked to step down when there is persistent:

  • Non-participation.
  • Failure to perform responsibilities.
  • Serious misconduct.
  • Unauthorized use of club resources.
  • Repeated violation of the Rulebook.

Except where immediate action is required to protect people, systems, or the club, the member should ordinarily have an opportunity to explain their circumstances.

The Lead, in consultation with relevant Core Team members, shall handle such matters.

27. RECOGNITION

The club may recognize members for:

  • Outstanding technical contributions.
  • Major project ownership.
  • Open-source contributions.
  • Event organization.
  • Community building.
  • Mentorship.
  • Outreach.
  • Consistent contribution.
  • Exceptional initiative.

Recognition may include certificates, acknowledgements, project ownership, leadership opportunities, or other appropriate forms of appreciation.

28. FINANCIAL & RESOURCE MANAGEMENT

Club resources must be used for legitimate club purposes.

Where applicable:

  • Expenses should be documented.
  • Purchases should have a clear purpose.
  • Club equipment should be maintained responsibly.
  • Financial decisions should follow institute procedures.
  • Members should not personally profit from club resources without appropriate authorization.

29. SOCIAL MEDIA & PUBLIC REPRESENTATION

Only authorized members with approval from Lead should make official statements on behalf of FOSS Club IIIT Kalyani.

Official accounts should:

  • Verify information before publishing.
  • Avoid misleading claims.
  • Respect privacy.
  • Credit creators.
  • Maintain club standards.
  • Coordinate major announcements with relevant Core Team members.

30. LEADERSHIP TRANSITION

Every Core Team should prepare the next generation.

Before the end of a leadership cycle, the outgoing team should, where practical:

  • Document ongoing projects.
  • Transfer relevant knowledge.
  • Transfer administrative access.
  • Document infrastructure.
  • Record unfinished work.
  • Train incoming members.
  • Preserve important institutional knowledge.

The goal is:

The club should outlive its individual leaders.

31. CORE TEAM CULTURE

Every Core Team member should embody five principles:

BUILD

Create things people can use.

CONTRIBUTE

Give back to the open-source ecosystem.

COLLABORATE

Work across teams and learn from one another.

TEACH

Share knowledge rather than gatekeeping it.

SUSTAIN

Build systems and documentation that future members can continue.

33. THE FOSS STANDARD

Before starting a project:

Why are we building this?

While building:

Are we building it well and collaboratively?

Before releasing:

Can another person understand, use, and contribute to it?

After releasing:

How can we improve it?

34. FINAL PRINCIPLE

FOSS Club IIIT Kalyani is not defined merely by its titles, events, social media presence, or number of members.

It is defined by what its members:

BUILD.

CONTRIBUTE.

COLLABORATE.

SHARE.

SUSTAIN.

The Core Team carries the responsibility of building a culture where students can explore technology, contribute to open source, collaborate with others, and create work that continues beyond their time at IIIT Kalyani.

BUILD. CONTRIBUTE. COLLABORATE.
